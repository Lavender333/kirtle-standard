Meet the woman behind the standard

Years of experience.
A personal touch.

For Ms.Kirtle, a clean home is about helping people feel comfortable, cared for, and ready to enjoy their space.

With years of hands-on cleaning experience, Ms.Kirtle brings care, attention to detail, and pride in her work to Kirtle Standard. She understands that every home has its own routine, and every client has different priorities.

Her approach begins with listening: which rooms need attention, what matters most to you, and how cleaning can make your day a little easier. From everyday upkeep to a more detailed clean, she focuses on thoughtful service shaped around your home.

Kirtle Standard reflects her commitment to doing the work with care, communicating clearly, and treating each space with respect. Serving Cleveland and Northeast Ohio, Ms.Kirtle looks forward to helping you create a home that feels fresh, welcoming, and easier to manage.
