First-time setup

  1. Log in to Square or create a business account and enable Appointments.
  2. Add your cleaning services, accurate durations, prices, service area, and availability. Set the business time zone to Eastern Time.
  3. Activate payment processing and choose your booking payment policy. Square’s documented prepayment option is full prepayment for fixed-price services; do not assume it collects a 50% deposit. Features depend on your Square plan.
  4. In Appointments → Online Booking → Channels, enable booking and copy the booking flow URL from Get URL.
  5. Add that public link as bookingUrl in assets/booking-config.js in the website repository, then publish the change. No secret keys are needed.
  6. Test the client booking flow, the service price, availability, and payment policy before sharing the link.
